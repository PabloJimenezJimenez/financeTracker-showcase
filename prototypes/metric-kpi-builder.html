Metric and KPI builder

Which pieces does the builder need, and how do they combine? Build Metrics from pieces (what to count, which Transactions, how to measure, over which period), combine saved Metrics, and pin one as a KPI with a target and a direction. The numbers come from four months of made-up Transactions, July to 20 October 2026.

What the model holds now

What this prototype proposes

  1. Four pieces per Metric: start from Spending or Income, narrow by Category, Merchant and Account (any of each), measure (sum, count, or an average), and a period.
  2. Combining is its own kind of Metric: ratio (A ÷ B), difference (A − B) or change against the previous period, over saved Metrics. Combined Metrics can be combined again, which is how a savings rate gets built.
  3. A combined Metric sets one period for both sides; the period saved on each side is ignored.
  4. Units are checked: only Metrics of the same unit can be subtracted; money ÷ money and count ÷ count give a percentage; money ÷ count gives money per transaction; everything else is refused.
  5. A KPI is a pinned Metric with an optional target in the Metric's own unit and an optional direction. For sums and counts in an unfinished period, status uses the pace projection; without a target, the direction compares with the previous period.
